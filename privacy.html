Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026

This policy explains what information Haynes Engineering & Excavation (“we”, “us”) collects through haynes2e.com and how it is used.

Information you send us

When you use our contact form, we receive the information you enter: your name, company or agency, email address, phone number and project details. The form is processed by our form provider, Web3Forms, which delivers your message to our company email.

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and to prepare estimates, bids and project communications. We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not add you to marketing lists.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use this website, such as which pages are viewed, the general region visitors come from, and the type of device and browser used. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect this information on our behalf. It does not give us your name or contact details.

You can learn how Google uses this data at How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or your browser's cookie settings.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security and operational purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to other sites

Our site links to other websites, such as Facebook. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Children

This website is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page.

Contact
